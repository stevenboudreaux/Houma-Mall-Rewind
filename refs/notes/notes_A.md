Batch: videos IMG_4406–4416 and IMG_4426; photos IMG_4417–4424 and IMG_4427–4436.
Frame fNNN is about (NNN−1)×2 s into its video. "Lens interiors" in the narration is **Lynn's Interiors**; the script "Lynn's" and the word INTERIORS show on the sign, the price tags and the box truck. "Cuccos" is **Cucos**.

## Summary (structural answers to the brief's questions)

- **H1a/H1b Sears halls (Cucos → cinema): Demo 41 is right.** Both hall ends have a flat 2×2 lay-in drop ceiling with small recessed cans and a 6-dome camera bar. The height is about 4.3–4.6 m: the storefront/facade band tops out at about 3.0 m and there is about 1.3–1.5 m of plain cream wall above it. Tiles look white near the east entrance and beige/tan in the cinema wing; that may be tile color or shade. Neither hall has a vault.
  - **New detail:** each hall has a short run of **3 crosswise deep skylight wells**. Each well is about 1.2–1.5 × 3.5–4 m, with splayed white drywall sides and a small **barrel-arched acrylic skylight** on top (IMG_4414 f008).
  - In H1b the wells are about 15 m in from the east entrance. In H1a they are at the court end, just west of the Sears court arch.
- **C1 Sears court: the ceiling does NOT drop.**
  - The ceiling is a cream/warm-beige **shallow barrel vault** whose axis runs N–S, with a long rectangular **lantern skylight** down the centre. The lantern is about 2.5 × 15 m: a deep white well with about 10 white crosswise baffle beams, glazed above.
  - Tall **clerestory strip windows** run along the E and W sides above a white fascia.
  - The north end wall (lunette over Lynn's) carries a **salmon/coral-pink stripe that follows the arch curve**, with a chrome art-deco fan sconce at the keystone. This confirms the 2018 "white arch beam with pink stripe".
  - Crown height is roughly 8–10 m.
  - Each hall enters the court through a **white segmental-arch soffit** (about 0.9 m deep). There is one on the east (H1b), one on the west (H1a) and one on the south (H2).
- **H2 north end (and H3, H4 in the photos): flat white 2×2 lay-in with modern linear LED strips** running across the hall about every 1.2–2.4 m (IMG_4408 f027/f041, IMG_4419, IMG_4424, IMG_4436). **This contradicts "ribbed barrel vaults over the main halls" from 2018,** at least for these stretches. Either the halls were re-ceilinged after 2018 or the vaults are hidden or only in other halls. The segmental arch soffits at the court edges survive.
- **Floors (consistent across the wing):**
  - Field: almond/cream 6-in (≈15 cm) square quarry-look tile, laid square to the hall, in 2–2.5 m lanes along each wall.
  - Centre "argyle" rug: blocks of 3×3 small tiles alternating charcoal-gray and almond/white, rotated 45°, with thin continuous dark diagonal grout lines through the block corners, plus a few single tan accent tiles. The rug ends in a pointed diamond near the hall ends.
  - Hall thresholds: a **terracotta/rust diamond-on-charcoal border band**, and single terracotta-diamond inset tiles near walls.
  - C1 court rug: terracotta + white argyle in the centre, framed by a charcoal/gray/white plaid band. The checkerboard ring around the planters is terracotta + white.
  - Main entrance and C2-area courts use a **sage-green + white** checker. The main-entrance threshold is **slate-blue + white**.
- **Old Sears interior (Lynn's Interiors):**
  - Floor: polished bare gray concrete with old mastic ghosts.
  - Ceiling: white 2×2 lay-in at about 3.6–3.8 m with 2×4 deep-cell parabolic troffers on a grid, plus wide white **dropped soffit beams** (about 1.2 m wide × 0.5 m deep) running along the column lines.
  - Columns: square, white, **about 0.65–0.75 m, on about a 9 m (30 ft) grid**.
  - Back zone near the bedding/mattress gallery: slender white round/square steel pipe columns (about 0.25 m), suggesting a different structural bay.
  - Partition walls (the "wall separators" Steven says "have always been there"): white panelized gypsum, about 1.2 m panels with vertical joints, a reveal at about 2.4 m, and a soffit ledge at about 3.1–3.2 m carrying **small white triangular corbel brackets about every 1.2 m**.
  - **No escalator, stair, void or second-floor opening was seen anywhere.** As filmed, the store reads as single-level.
  - The main aisle runs straight N from the mall entrance on the C1 axis. Looking back S from inside, the aisle lines up exactly with H2, and the Dillard's sign is visible about 200 m away (IMG_4413 f009–010).
