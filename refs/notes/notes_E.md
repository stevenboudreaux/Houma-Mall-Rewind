I viewed every frame in all four videos: 4490 f001–f083, 4491 f001–f008, 4492 f001–f110 and 4493 f001–f044. I also enlarged the Dillard's directory (4490 f074/f076) to read it.

**Main finding:** the "old, untouched map" inside Dillard's is a map of **Dillard's own departments**. It lists no mall tenants, so it cannot be used as a 2026 tenant source. The full text is below.

**Orientation note.** I used the game's compass throughout: Sears is north, Dillard's south, the main entrance on the east side facing out to West Park Ave, and JCPenney west. Coming out of the main entrance, Steven's 4492 walk toward Dillard's goes **south** (to his right as he faces out). His 4493 walk "to the left" goes **north** toward K&B, Cucos and Sears.
