# notes_D — Exterior batch: JCPenney, west/back lot, Bayou Gardens side entrance, Dillard's wing (Oct 23 2026)

Videos: IMG_4487 (54 frames, ~108 s), IMG_4488 (26 frames, ~52 s), IMG_4489 (83 frames, ~166 s). I viewed every frame.

**How the shots are oriented.** None of this footage shows a compass, so I worked out directions from three things: Steven's narration ("Bayou Gardens Boulevard" is the road with palms seen in 4489), the strip center and drive-thru bank (both southwest of JCPenney), and the soccer fields and houses (west). On that basis:
- Bayou Gardens Blvd runs along the **south**.
- The "back lot" from 4487 is **west** of JCPenney (the rear/staff lot behind the west wing).
- JCPenney's long blank wall with the wordmark and podocarpus hedge (4488) is its **west face**. The red arcade and second wordmark (4489) are on its **south face**.
- Walking east along the south face reaches the mall's peach entrance pavilion (= 1995 **H6 / s55 EXIT**, x75–79 y144, next to Terrebonne General Outreach s7b and Corn Dog 7 s54). Then comes a long low peach wing wall (the back of the H5 south row, County Seat … Regis, y≈150). That wall ends at **Dillard's west end wall** (x129, y140–159).

This all fits the 1995 map. The one placement I'm not sure of is the package-pickup entrance in 4487: I put it on JCPenney's **north** face (medium confidence).
