# Batch B: south concourse, Dillard's court, JCPenney end (2026-10-23 visit)

Videos IMG_4437–4444, 4457–4459, 4461 and photos 4445, 4455–4483 (subset). I viewed every frame and photo. Sizes are estimates from the frames: 12" (30 cm) floor tile is the main scale reference, and the 1995 rect depth of 20 tiles is about 40 m.

## Batch-wide structural findings (read first)

1. **Hall ceilings in 2026 are flat, not vaulted.** H4, H5 and H6 all have a flat white 2x2 lay-in acoustic ceiling (ACT). Continuous linear LED strips run lengthwise in 4–6 rows. The ceiling sits about 4.6–5.0 m up, and storefront bulkheads top out around 3.6–4.0 m. At intervals of about 25–30 m the hall is crossed by **segmental arched gypsum soffit bands**, painted pale grey-blue/celadon, with the arch rising about 0.6 m from springing to crown. Over H5 east of AT&T there is a **square pyramidal skylight** (white aluminium, hipped 4-sided, about 3.5 × 3.5 m) set in a cream stepped coffer, with recessed cans in the coffer ring (4440 f4, 4442).
   - This contradicts the 2018 "ribbed white barrel vault + checker band" description for these halls. Either the halls were re-ceilinged after 2018, or the 2018 vaults were on other halls.
   - For 1995, keep the 2018 vault. For a 2026 toggle, use the flat ACT and LED look, which is the same language Demo 41 used for the Sears halls.
2. **The courts keep their vaults.**
   - **C3 (Dillard's court):** butter/cream plaster barrel vault with cove uplight and a square skylight well at the crown. White arch beams carry a **navy-blue stripe** on the soffit edge, with champagne Art-Deco **stepped-fan sconces** (3 tiers plus a ball, about 1 m tall) on the beams. The court ring is a flat cream gypsum ceiling with linear slot diffusers and downlights.
   - **C4 (JCPenney court):** butter-yellow segmental barrel vault with a long rectangular **monitor skylight** (white baffles) along the crown. A flat white gypsum beam ring with a cove LED uplight sits on **white round columns** (about 40 cm across) with a flared inverted-cone capital, 2 reveal grooves and a charcoal base band. The vault end tympanum is pale celadon/mint, also with stepped-fan sconces.
   - **C2 corner (4437):** high flat ACT ceiling (about 7 m), a bulkhead with a dark reveal line, and a skylight edge visible.
3. **Floors are unchanged from 1995.** All of them are glazed 12" ceramic tile.
   - Centre field: **diagonal checker** of mid-grey and white with random tan tiles.
   - Store lanes: about 3 m of **beige straight-laid** tile.
   - Lane accents: **terracotta-in-charcoal diamond inserts** (about 60 cm) at intervals, and **copper/terracotta + black diamond bands** at court thresholds.
   - C3 field: blue-grey/white diagonal checker.
   - C4 and the H5 skylight node: terracotta/grey/white straight checker field with charcoal borders.
4. **Original 1990s mall details to copy:**
   - Terracotta **8-point star medallion** (about 25 cm) at the top of the cream pilasters between stores, all along H5 and H6.
   - Cream tapered round trash cans.
   - Oak slat benches with an elliptical back, in facing pairs around a round cream planter with a palm.
   - Small white plaster planters.
   - Directory totems: grey aluminium frame, lit panel, champagne deco fan finial.
5. **Tile wainscot is gone.** Store piers in H5 are smooth cream drywall with an 8" beige tile base. The exception is the old Zales-style chocolate-brown wainscot at the C2 Zales.
