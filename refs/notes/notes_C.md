### IMG_4484 (~136 s, 68 frames): East hall H2, walked north from the C2/H3 corner (Claire's) to the Sears court C1 (x~145→140, y~97→55)

- **Route:** Starts in the C2 court at the NE corner where H2 meets H3 (f001–011). Pans east down H3 toward the main entrance (f010–011), then turns west across the court to the Billiard Room (f013–015). Walks north up H2 along the east side (Hypervelocity, then the vacant shops, f016–043). Crosses to the west side at SOLA / Prime Time Fitness (f044–056). Ends among the kiddie rides at the mouth of the Sears court, looking at "Lynn's Interiors" (f057–068).
- **Narration audio:** 0–55 s is narration. **[56 s] "back" is the only word in that window, so 56–84 s is effectively music/ambient.** Frames f029–f042 (gray/red vacant, "Feelin' Hungry?" wall, black-glass fronts) have no narration and are described from the frames only. 84–136 s is narration again.

**Structure**
- **H2 ceiling in 2026:** flat 2×2 white lay-in acoustic tile at about 5 m (roughly 1.5–2 m above the storefront sign bands). Continuous linear LED slot lights run *across* the hall about 2.4 m apart. Round 4-way supply diffusers sit in clusters of 5–6 (f017–019, f044). **No ribbed barrel vault is visible anywhere in H2.** This contradicts the 2018 walkthrough: the vault has probably been covered by a later dropped ceiling. For 1995, keep the vault.
- **Store-lane soffit:** about 3.3–3.6 m, cream drywall with round can downlights. It steps up to the hall ceiling in 1–2 tiers, and the upper wall is painted pale sage/gray-green (f041–043).
- **Hall-end bulkheads:** where H2 meets C2 and C1 there are segmental-arch soffit bulkheads, about 0.8 m deep, in cream at the C2 end (f003–004). At the Sears end the bulkhead is a pale gray-green **scalloped/wavy** band (f018–020, f043–044).
- **C2 corner court (f001–005):** lower flat 2×2 ceiling of about 4 m with recessed cans. White round columns about 0.45 m in diameter with a 0.3 m gray-tile collar at the base. Dark-stained wood slat benches stand back-to-back around a round cream planter, and there are tapered cream trash cans.
- **Pilasters:** every demising pier on both sides is a cream pilaster about 0.3 m wide with a **terracotta 8-point star medallion** (two overlapping 25 cm squares) at about 3 m (f013, f016, f021, f042, f046, f062). The 1995 model should copy this. Several stores have white fluted Doric half-columns (f039–042). Prime Time and SOLA share large glossy white tapered columns with bulbous turned capitals (f046–049).
- **Floors:**
  - C2 court field: sage-green/white checker laid on the diagonal (f001–005).
  - Ring band: charcoal/gray/cream plaid stripes.
  - H2 centre: argyle diagonal checker in gray, cream and occasional beige.
  - Store lanes: cream 8" square tile, about 2 m wide, with loose diamond insets every ~6 m (a 1.2 m charcoal frame with a terracotta centre; f011, f039, f043).
  - Where the hall field meets the courts there is a **sawtooth border** of terracotta and charcoal triangles (f013–016, f044).
  - At the Sears court the rug changes to a terracotta/white checker with a charcoal/gray/white band (f046–048).
  - Condition is good and polished. This is clearly the original 1980s–90s tile.
- **Sears court C1 (f068):** cream segmental vault with a square skylight and a clerestory strip, and a salmon/pink-striped arch beam with a cream art-deco fan medallion at the crown. This **matches the 2018 description and does not drop.** Also present: a big palm in a white tapered planter, a white directory pylon with a fan crest and pink band, and black lantern sconces flanking the Sears opening. Only the halls dropped, so Demo 41's flat 4.6 m drop is right for the halls but not for C1.

**Storefronts / tenants (south → north)**
- **E side, corner H2/H3: CLAIRE'S** (f007–011). Purple lowercase channel letters "claire's" on a white fascia, a silver crumpled-metal trim band, and an all-glass storefront in white mullions that wraps the corner with fronts on both H2 and H3 (the "double end cap"). Steven: "the end cap… that was like the jewelry… it's double end cap; lower ceiling drop right there." In 1995 this was Gordon's Jewelers (s29) at the corner with Claire's (s30) east of it, so Claire's now appears to take the corner. **Re-cut** (see table).
- **E side: NEEK & CO.** (f002–006, f012, f021). Bright blue bold sans "Neek" and gold script "&Co." on a white crown-moulded fascia with applied cream arch moulding. Gray/rose veined marble-look tile pilasters about 0.9 m wide. Black spiral-rod roll grille about 10 m wide, plus a narrower clear grille bay. Black-framed glass at the north end. A clothing boutique.
- **E side: HYPERVELOCITY RACE TRACK** (f017–030). Silver chrome italic "HYPERVELOCITY / RACE TRACK" on a red sign band about 1.5 m tall and 20 m+ long. Below: three fixed windows about 3 × 1.1 m in black frames, a 0.4 m white band, and black lower panels (race-livery look). Inside is a big open floor; "Inspired 2 Dance" signage reflects in the glass.
  - At the north end is a full-height white sculpted portal with a **semicircular notch cut down from its top**, two black pinstripes, and a black door: "USE EXTERIOR ENTRANCE TO VISIT THE RACE TRACK."
  - Steven: this was **K&B**; "that little under the circle up top, that's where the K&B logo lies… the mirrors… fluorescent lighting." The notched portal is the old K&B logo niche. The mirror front itself is gone (now black/red panels).
  - It occupies the K&B box (s25) through to the back; there is an exterior entrance.
- **E side, N of Hypervelocity: VACANT** (f028–034). A canted gray-blue box sign band with a red underside and about 7 cans, a full-width silver grid grille about 7 m wide, and a **navy-blue tile threshold**. The pier has stacked gray/red/gray/navy blocks, and inside are navy walls with slatwall frames. This is an old athletic-shoe livery in the s24/s24mc/n65 zone (Philly's / Mastercuts / Father & Son). The frames suggest a Foot Locker-family store, but no name is legible.
- **E side: VACANT narrow front** (f035–038). Cream crown cornice, blank white sign panel, white panelled pilasters, beige 12" ceramic tile wall cladding, black roll grille with a louvred bottom, and a black drape. About 4 m wide.
- **E side: "Feelin' Hungry?" leasing wall** (f035–042). Blue-painted plywood about 7 m × 3 m with a flush blue door and white fluted Doric half-columns on each side. Logos on it: Cakelicious, Great American Cookies, Auntie Anne's, The Red Seal Pizzeria & Bar, Corn Dog 7, Prepz, Cinnamonster, Wicked Wingz (a food-court directory). The space behind is vacant.
- **E side, toward C1: VACANT** (f042–043). Black smoked-mirror glass fronts, floor to soffit. Next to them is a cream stucco front with a stepped "ziggurat" relief and red-orange gable-outline trim over a narrow grille. Together these cover the n62/n63/s27/s28 zone. Steven (112 s): "I believe that was **Felger's** right there."
- **E side, in view from the rides: VACANT** (f057–058). A cream facade with a shallow **triangular pediment** relief over a cornice, a thin pink/red line at the head, and a folding chain/scissor grille. This is probably the "Felger's" Steven points at (n62, possibly with the unnamed n63). Medium confidence on which side; see below.
- **W side, across from Claire's/Neek: THE BILLIARD ROOM + APL dart venue** (f013–016). Gold serif caps "THE BILLIARD ROOM" on a black panel, a glossy black fascia, a black faux-marble square column at the entry, and an open front. Blue-felt pool tables inside, with Halloween caution tape. Next to it is frameless black-edged glass with APL dart-league banners (red/white/blue) and toe-line mats. Steven at about 28 s: "**that was Footaction**" (s14), probably extended into Kids Mart (s15).
- **W side: VACANT blue box** (f013–017). A royal-blue painted facade about 12 m with a 1.2 m deep return, one glass bay, a charcoal roll grille and a string of white star lights. This is the Kids Mart / Jean Nicole zone (s15/s16).
- **W side:** a narrow black front with glass doors (small sign, unreadable). Then a tan wood-veneer panel facade wrapped in purple vinyl reading "Walk this way for decorating" with Lynn's Interiors graphics (f017–018), which is a Lynn's annex or advertising wrap. Then a mint/teal-framed glass store.
- **W side: dance studio, teal fascia** (f047–048). White dancer silhouette decal, trophies, a white/gray striped pier. Its name is not on the fascia, but the "Inspired 2 Dance" banner seen through Hypervelocity's windows is probably this tenant. This is the s17 Miller's Outpost zone.
- **W side: PRIME TIME FITNESS ★★★★** (f046–049). Black script with four black stars on a white glossy 4" tile sign band whose lower edge is an **S-wave** cut over the opening, inside a black fascia frame. A big white tapered column with a bulbous capital, a silver grid grille and a black-tile threshold. Inside are a vending machine and an office. Steven (84 s): "those pillars… that used to be **Kay-Bee Toys**." That makes this s18.
- **W side: SOLA, South Louisiana Center for the Arts** (f044–045, f050–055). Multicolour logo (lime/magenta/blue/orange with drama masks and a treble clef) on black glass.
  - **Fascia:** a curved, bull-nosed **black textured-stucco band** about 0.8 m tall projecting about 0.4 m, with a white soffit underneath.
  - **Pilasters and glass:** **glossy black cylindrical pilasters** about 0.4 m in diameter, angled black-glass vestibule panels, and a gray wall with a flush gray door (the old entry, now walled).
  - **Floor:** a **black 8" tile** threshold.
  - Steven: "**sound shop**… it goes to black tile… curved… those black shiny pillars" (s19). The frames confirm this exactly. It is the best-preserved 1995 storefront in the batch.
- **W side, north of SOLA: VACANT, large** (f062–067). A white horizontal metal-panel bulkhead band about 1.5 m tall over a full-width silver grille about 15 m+ wide, with track lights inside and red "oh what fun / kids" placards left behind. Steven (112 s): "that's big. That probably was combined with like two different units." This is the n10 Lane Bryant + n9 Rave zone merged.
- **W side, end:** a recessed corridor with a white service/restroom door (f063–064). Then black glass panels between cream square columns with white wainscot bases, next to the Sears court (f064–065). Steven: "**Solarium** that used to be at the end. Or maybe it was open" (n8).
- Also seen (f059–061): a charcoal facade with a recessed moulded sign panel, three soffit downlights, a horizontal-slat grille, and a long blank charcoal wall with a flush door (a walled-up front). Its exact slot is unsure; it is near SOLA.
- **Sears front (s1): LYNN'S INTERIORS** (f043, f068). Black script "Lynn's" and black serif caps "INTERIORS" on a white wall. A wide open front into a furniture showroom with white square columns.

**Interiors:** none entered. Seen through glass:

**Steven's build directions:** none explicit. "Look at the structure. So I think all of those are okay" (~120 s), meaning he thinks the structure is unchanged.

### IMG_4485 (~70 s, 35 frames): Sears hall H1a (cinema wing), walked west from the Sears court to the west exit vestibule s41 (x~130→108, y52–57)

- **Route:** Walks west along the south wall from about x130 to the glass exit at x108–110, looking mostly left (south) at the wall, with brief looks at the ceiling and the north wall. Ends at the exit doors (f034–035).
- **Narration audio:** narration throughout. It is choppy: "oh no no no… this is the bank, duh" at ~40 s is Steven correcting himself. No music-only lines.

**Structure**
- **Ceiling:** a flat 2×2 white acoustic drop ceiling at about **4.5 m** (f001, f012), with sprinkler heads and recessed cans. A row of 4–6 round diffusers runs down the centre, plus track lights near the exit. There are **three rectangular skylight wells** punched into the drop ceiling (f001). **This confirms Demo 41's flat cream drop at about 4.6 m for the cinema hall.** Add the skylight wells.
- **North wall (Sears side):** painted cream **brick** with brick pilasters (f013). Steven: "the older brick wall, that's still the same." The north wall also has tall cream stucco piers (f012).
- **South wall (x~125–130, between the court and the bank):** a mock half-timber panel wall:
  - Textured cream stucco panels about 2.4 m wide × 2.7 m tall, framed in gray-painted rough-sawn wood trim.
  - Each panel has a black-framed **dark reflective "picture window"** about 1.3 m wide × 1.6 m tall with its sill at about 0.6 m. Steven is reflected in one (f021), so there is no display space behind it.
  - The panels sit on a gray-painted brick ledge/base about 0.5 m tall.
  - Black-and-white half-shaded round wall sconces are mounted between the panels.
  - Above the trim, plain cream wall runs up to the ceiling (f002–022).
- **Doors:** a gray steel door with a transom panel in a gray frame on the brick base (f023).
- **AMERICAN BANK facade (s29ab, f024–027):**
  - Painted-white textured faux brick.
  - A **large semicircular soldier-course brick arch** about 4.5 m wide with white infill, holding white double doors about 2 m wide.
  - A crown cornice above the arch at about 3.5 m, then a framed panel with pilaster caps.
- **CINEMA facade (s40, f031–033):**
  - White-painted brick wall with **tall round-headed blind arches**, each about 2.4 m wide × 4 m tall with white panel infill. One arch holds a small white door.
  - Blank white square poster boards about 1.2 m between the arches.
  - Steven: "three arches for the movie theater." The frames show the arches clearly, though the count is hard to confirm from the angle.
- **West end, s41 exit (f034–035):** a full-height aluminium glass curtain wall with 4 pairs of aluminium doors and exit signs. Outside: the tan/red brick cinema mass (left), the lot and a light pole.
- **Floor:** cream square lane tile continues. The hall-centre pattern is mostly out of frame.

**Storefronts / tenants:** none open. The bank is VACANT and walled; Steven: "they obviously got rid of the bank that was right here." The cinema is closed; its arches are blind.

**Steven's build directions**
- ~0 s: "Take note, see these little picture windows… those are probably always been in there. Those are where the **ads** were, so maybe you can do that and then we can crop." He wants the dark windows used as ad frames (backlit ad panels in 1995).
- ~10 s: "Look at the light fixtures." These are the half-shaded round sconces on the panel wall.
- ~50 s: "This is an old facade… really the only things left are this brick." Keep the brick arch.

**Best frames**
- f001: hall perspective with drop ceiling and skylight wells.
- f008: picture-window panel with sconce (ad-frame texture).
- f013: original north brick wall.
- f025: bank brick arch.
- f032: cinema blind arches.
- f035: west exit vestibule.

**Confidence / uncertainty**
- Whether the picture windows are 1995 originals: Steven says "probably always been." The half-timber look reads as a late-1980s theming.
- The exact x-extent of the panel wall versus the Solarium/Rave side walls.

### IMG_4486 (232 s, 116 frames): Exterior. Out the s41 west exit, around the cinema's west wall, across the staff lot behind the H5 north row, past the 1970 water tank, to the JCPenney north wall/docks and its NW corner

- **Route:**

  2. Walks south along the cinema's west wall on a ramped walk (f012–035).
  3. "Plants" in the lot at about x95–100, y75–90 for a 360° pan (f036–076).
  4. Walks SW on the sidewalk past the fenced water tank (f077–102).
  5. Reaches the JCPenney north wall and loading docks 1–2 (f103–112) at about x60–70, y114.
  6. Walks west along JCP's north wall to its NW corner and the customer lot (f113–116).
- **Narration audio:** narration 0–223 s. **[224 s] "(static)" is ambient/wind only.** Frames f113–116 still show the JCP NW corner and the west lot.

**Exterior / parking**

  - Yellow pipe rail on a hot-pink-painted curb.

  - A Lynn's box truck ("NOW ON SALE").
  - Steven: "That was Sears loading for all of their trucks… take note of this."
  - A separate long low white warehouse box lies farther north (f010, f072); Steven: "that building back there used to be Sears… original 1969 standalone building."
- **Cinema exterior, west wall (f014–037, f069–072):**
  - Red/brown brick about 9–10 m tall with a flat parapet and metal coping.
  - **Peach-painted stucco pilasters** about 0.5 m wide every 8–10 m, and a peach base band of about 0.6 m.
  - Beige steel exit doors with small hoods and wall-pack lights every few bays (theatre exits; Steven: "where you also came out of the movie theater").

  - The west exit vestibule at its NE corner: glass curtain wall under a flat tan aggregate-panel fascia band, between the white Sears wall (N) and the cinema brick (S) (f014–015).
  - A ramped walk with three steps, a dark pipe rail and a **peach concrete knee wall** about 1 m (Steven: "the rail, that was it").

  - Long low tan-brown brick wall about 6–7 m tall with a dark soldier-course line at about 4.5–5 m.

  - Steven: "all of the back entrances to the stores… on the side of Gadzooks… this is all staff parking."

  - Jointed concrete panels about 6 × 6 m (not asphalt), with storm grates and faded yellow single and hairpin stall stripes.
  - Yellow-curbed teardrop end islands and raised sidewalk islands with rounded curb noses.
  - Yellow hatched no-park zones and blue accessible stalls near JCP.
  - A white "STOP" stencil, pink-hatched crosswalks, and a double yellow centreline on the ring drive (~7–8 m wide).
  - Light poles: modern LED in single, twin curved-arm and quad cross-arm heads, on **yellow-painted square/round concrete bases**. These are 2020s fixtures; for 1995, use period shoebox heads on the same base positions.
  - Fire hydrants: red, and yellow with blue caps.

- **Landscaping (f083–116):**
  - A lawn island with a large **southern magnolia** and tall **loblolly pines**.
  - Raised concrete-curbed beds with liriope/ivy groundcover and mulch.
  - Clipped holly and ligustrum balls.
  - A foundation bed of bare-pruned crape myrtles along the JCP wall.
- **JCPENNEY north wall and docks (f103–113):**
  - White-painted brick (running bond) about 9 m tall with flat metal coping, raw concrete corner pilasters, wall packs, and a dome security camera on a mast at the roof corner.
  - **Red "JCPenney" channel letters** (modern mixed-case logo with black returns) mounted high, mid-wall. Modern; for 1995, use the period logo.
  - **Two dock doors numbered 1 and 2:** white roll-ups about 3 × 3 m with black dock-seal shelters and yellow bumper strips, a raised dock of about 1.2 m, concrete steps with a galvanized rail, a man door, and a **red corrugated metal canopy band** about 1 m deep over the docks.
