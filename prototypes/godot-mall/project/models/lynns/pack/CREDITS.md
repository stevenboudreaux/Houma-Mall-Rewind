# Lynn's Interiors pack furniture: sources and licence

The `pk_*.gltf` + `.bin` models in this folder are converted (rescaled, re-oriented, recoloured onto `ly_palette.png`,
joined into one mesh) by `tools/blender/import_quaternius.py` from two CC0 packs Steven supplied on Nov 4 2026.
Both are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)**,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Credit is not required; it is given here with thanks.

- **Ultimate House Interior Pack** (June 2020) by **Quaternius** — https://quaternius.com ,
  https://www.patreon.com/quaternius . Source files: `FBX/<Name>.fbx` (named per model in
  `design/lynns/asset-manifest.md`).
- **Furniture Kit 2.0** (2018) by **Kenney** — https://www.kenney.nl . Source files:
  `Models/GLTF format/<name>.glb`.

`pk_mat_*`, `pk_pillowtower` and `pk_rug_round` are built by the conversion script itself (no pack source).
The palette textures `ly_palette.png` / `ly_palette_orm.png` are generated by the same script.
Only the models placed in the store are kept in the repository.
