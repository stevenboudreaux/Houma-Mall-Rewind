# RadioShack 1995 prices (research note)

**Purpose:** typical 1995 US retail prices for each kind of item the 1995 RadioShack store shows
(`design/storefronts/radio-shack.md`, `prototypes/godot-mall/project/tools/stores/small/paint_rs.py`,
`radio_shack.gd`), for price tags and signs. In the game every item is the invented brand **VOLTARO**.
Real model names and Cat. Nos. below are for reference only and must not appear in the game.

**Researched:** Nov 3, 2026.

**Read this first: nothing here is verified against a page.** This session's network policy refused
every source host (WebFetch/curl): `worldradiohistory.com`, `www.radioshackcatalogs.com`, `archive.org`,
`www.radiomuseum.org`, `www.rigpix.com`, `digital.bentley.umich.edu`, `scholar-dev.cloud.lib.vt.edu`
and `cedmagic.com`. Only web search worked. So no row is **sourced** from the web; the pager row comes from a local ad in Steven's refs pack, read directly. Every other row is one of these:

- **lead**: a price that a search engine's summary attributes to the cited URL, often quoting that
  page's own catalog citation. I did not open the page. Treat it as a strong lead, and confirm it
  before calling it sourced.
- **guess**: no period source found. The reasoning is in Notes.

To finish the job, allow the hosts above in the session's network settings (environment, then
Network access, then Allowed domains), then open the 1995 catalog (radioshackcatalogs.com) or the

## Guesses and gaps

- **Leads only, no sourced rows:** mobile CB $99.99 (TRC-483), the scanner range around the Pro-24 at
  $179.99, the micro-cassette premium at $99.99 (1996), and DSS at $699/$899 (not RadioShack-specific).
  Each comes from a search summary of the cited page, which I did not open.
- **Read directly:** the pager price ($79.99 sale, $6.95/mo), from a local Radiofone ad in Steven's refs pack.
- **Pure guesses:** walkie-talkies, R/C truck, 900 MHz and basic cordless phones, AA 4-pack,
  corded phone, answering machine, clock radio, cell phone, batteries, parts, AC adapter, wire,
  monster truck, computers, boxed goods. They follow RS's ".99" pricing and general 1995 market levels.
- **Open questions for `mall-historian`:** did a 1995 RadioShack carry DSS? Did it still give the
  battery card? What did its "Computers" section hold in 1995 (Tandy had sold its PC maker to AST
  in 1993)?
- **Blocked:** every source host. Allowing `worldradiohistory.com` alone (the 1993, 1996 and 1998 catalog
  PDFs are there) would let the next pass verify most rows.
