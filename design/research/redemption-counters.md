# Redemption prize counters, 1990s and 2020s (research note)

**Purpose:** how real arcade prize counters are laid out, so the 1995 Pocket Change counter
(`design/godot-prize-counter.md`, `scripts/prizes.json`) and the 2026 games, inc. "GALACTIC PRIZE
PORTAL" (`design/storefronts/games-inc-2026.md`, P1-P6) can be filled to look real. This is a list of
**options for Steven to choose from**, not a build or a decision. Items and ticket tiers are in
`design/research/redemption-items.md`.

**Researched:** Nov 3, 2026.

## 2. How a counter is put together (summary; leads + guesses)

| Part | 1990s (Pocket Change) | 2020s (games, inc.) | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Showcase | Wood-grain or black laminate base, glass top and front, 2-3 glass shelves, fluorescent tube inside | White or black base, glass, LED strip edges (colour-changing), white interior LED, sliding rear doors | 1990s: Steven's photos + **guess**; 2020s: L15 |
| Under the glass | Rows of same-size open acrylic bins (or a divided tray), one prize per bin, a standing price card in or in front of each bin | Same bins, plus small boxed and carded items standing in rows; blind boxes stacked | L5, L16; rows/one-per-bin: Steven's photos; rest **guess** |
| Grading | Cheapest within reach of the shortest kids and where the clerk grabs fastest; dearer items on the more visible shelf | Same; tiers also by shelf height on the wall | L5, L8 |
| Back wall | Pegboard or slatwall with hooks; plush hung by tags in rows; big boxed items (boom box, radio-control car) on a top shelf | Black or white slatwall, 4-6 shelves of boxed items and plush; jumbo plush on top ("Above All") | L9, L12, L17; 1990s: Steven's photos + **guess** |
| Price tags | White card, big red number, "TICKETS" | Shelf-edge strips or clip tags, one colour (green at games, inc.) | 1990s: our cards (prizes.json art); 2020s: games-inc-2026.md P1 |
| Fullness | "full at all times" | "front and face"; a modern guide still wants 20-30 % air per shelf | L4, L6 |
| Light | Fluorescent in cases + 3 brighter cans over the counter (our build) | 4000-5000 K LED, spots on the top tier, LED edges | L7; 1990s **guess** |
| Tickets | Paper strips, counted by a ticket-eater machine or by weight | E-tickets on a game card; kiosks to check balance | L18; games-inc P6 |

**Steven's two rules, met how (proposal):**
1. *Under the glass: neat bins, nothing strewn.* Every level is a single row of identical bins
   (same footprint, same height, flush to the front glass), each holding one prize kind, its price
   card standing at the front centre. Loose items only *inside* a bin. Boxed or carded items stand in
   a straight row against a bin-width riser, never lying flat on the deck.
2. *The back wall looks full.* No bare panel larger than about 30 × 30 cm (**guess** threshold);
   hooks in a regular grid with items hanging two or three deep; shelves filled end to end; the top
   edge broken by plush and big boxes. The 20-30 % air of L6 applies to shelf *depth*, not to gaps
   along the wall.

## 3. Proposal (a): Pocket Change, 1995

**Fixtures as built** (`design/godot-prize-counter.md`): long case A, 4 bays × 3 levels (0 deck,
1 lower shelf, 2 upper shelf; slots `full`/`left`/`right`); short case B by the counter gate,
1 bay × 3 levels; 5 m prize wall with a base cabinet, a middle shelf, a top shelf, hooks along the top.
Bays 0-3 run from the customer's left.

**Grading rule (proposal):** price rises **left to right by bay** (bay 0 cheapest) and **upward by
level** inside a bay (deck = the cheaper half of that bay's band, upper shelf = the dearer half,
because the upper shelf is nearest the eye through the glass top). Case B, by the gate, is the
"impulse" case: candy only, so the clerk at the gate reaches it fastest (**guess**, from L5's
"behind the counter for staff").

To keep bins neat and full, an **option** is a third slot width (`left`/`mid`/`right`, three bins per
level) wherever a two-bin level looks sparse.

## 4. Proposal (b): games, inc., 2026

**Fixtures** (`design/storefronts/games-inc-2026.md` P1-P6): black slatwall prize wall ~8 m long,
2.2 m tall, 6 shelves, green price tags, plush on top; three LED-edged glass showcases 1.83 × 0.56 ×
0.97 m (pink and blue at the front, white at the far end); the black register counter between them;
two card kiosks; an orange wall behind. 1.83 m is close to the 70" stock counter of L15 (lead), so a
three-shelf glass case with an LED-edged front fits the period (**guess**).
