# Lynn's Interiors (the old Sears, 2026): furniture inventory from the footage — workstream LYNNS, phase 1 (Nov 4 2026)

**Asked for (Steven, walkthrough voice note Nov 3, `refs/notes/transcripts/walkthrough1.txt`):** "the
interiors of Lynn's Interiors … the video shows all of the beds and all the sofas in great detail, and in
the game it's sparse and nothing looks correct … get every object, and have a few variants of the styles
of bedroom sets and the styles of sofas and everything, based on the analysis."

This note is the research: every zone and object type seen, with counts, sizes, colours and frames, and
the style families (3–4 variants each) the build works from. The floor plan is `layout.md`
(+ `layout.png`); the build plan, budgets and estimate are in `plan.md`. The skeleton (walls,
columns, rooms) is workstream E's (`design/godot-sears.md`) and is not changed by this work.

- Frames: `refs/new/sears/IMG_44xx_t000_NNN.jpg` (1080 × 1920, 2 per second: t000_NNN ≈ (NNN−1)/2 s).
  Every second of all five videos was read (contact sheets of the odd frames), then the best frames for
  each family at full size. `refs/derived/frames/IMG_44xx/fNNN.jpg` are the older 1-per-2 s cuts.
- **How sizes were measured.** Against things of known size in the same frame: the square columns
  (0.70 m), the 1.2 m wall panels with the reveal at 2.4 m and the ledge at 3.15 m, the 2 × 2 ft (0.61 m)
  ceiling tiles and 2 × 4 ft troffers, the 9 m column grid, the 0.6 m concrete scuff lines. Sizes are
  ±10 %; where a standard US retail size agrees (queen mattress 60 × 80 in, three-seat sofa 84–92 in), the
  standard is used.
- **Counts.** "Seen" = distinct pieces counted in the footage (a piece seen in several frames counted
  once). "Store" = an estimate for the whole floor: the footage covers about 60 % of the 3,500 m² (the
  west room R2 and the NW room R1 are seen only in passing), so the seen count is scaled up zone by zone
  where a zone is only partly filmed. Treat "Store" as the number to build for, not a fact.
- **IP.** Real brand marks are visible on the mattress slatwall, the adjustable bases and the mattress
  panels. None are reproduced: they become invented marks (`plan.md` "Signage"). The Lynn's script mark on
  the hang tags is the tenant's own and is allowed.

## The store as filmed (finishes and fixtures that frame the furniture)

- **Floor:** polished light-grey concrete, scuffed with drag marks and old mastic ghosts (already built);
  the mattress gallery on a field of **taupe loop carpet** with a hard edge (4412 t023–045); floor boxes
  (two round brass-capped receptacles) in the carpet and in the dining area (4412 t042, t065).
- **Walls:** white panel walls with the corbelled ledge; the vignette alcoves along the perimeter under it
  (4413 t029, t089, t120). A **white horizontal slatwall** behind the mattress alcove under a stepped
  soffit (4412 t042–043, 4413 t119) and behind drapery samples.
- **Ceiling:** stained return grilles over the living area (4413 t099–t141, brown water rings): already a
  ceiling detail, noted for the ceiling pass.
- **On the columns:** fire extinguishers on brackets and red fire-department placards (4413 t129–135),
  a slotted standard strip; a black tower fan at the front (4413 t129).
- **Lighting:** only the ceiling troffers; every lamp in the store is merchandise (off).

## Style families and their variants

The families are built as **one generator each with parameters** (`plan.md`), so a variant is a set of
numbers and finishes. Each family gets 3–4 variants from what the footage shows most; more variants are
cheap once a generator exists.
