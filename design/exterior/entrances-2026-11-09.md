## The main entrance, measured (Nov 11 2026, EXT-ENTRANCES-BUILD)

**Why the Nov 9 massing was wrong.** It scaled the tower to the door's 12 m and to "8 m out from the facade" (the
door record's depth), and its side-by-side with f003 already showed a much stockier thing than the frame. Building it
"for good" against the frames (Steven: "make sure you get the heightened area perfect") meant measuring first.

**How it was measured (4406 f003, 960 x 1706).** The frame has two clean sets of parallel horizontals: north-south
(the curb, the facade's parapet, the slab's front edge) and east-west (the slab's north edge). Their vanishing points
(x -405 and x 2779 px on a horizon at y 816) give the focal length (1426 px, a 37 degree horizontal field: an
iPhone main camera in video) and the heading: the camera looks 31.8 degrees west of south. With the camera 1.7 m up
(which makes the piers' five bands 0.55 m, the Nov 9 figure) every ground point and every point at a known height
then has a position. Checks: the slab's two visible edges come out at right angles to 3 decimals, the facade's
pilaster bases come out on one north-south line 4.1 and 4.3 m apart (the game's 4 m bays), and a render from the
solved camera lands the slab, the cornices, the pylon and the facade on the frame (`wsEXTE-main-2026.jpg`, row 1).
