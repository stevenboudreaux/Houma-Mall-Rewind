# B. Dalton Bookseller: front and store (1995 map, store s48)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (level 3, from Steven's photos): "a deep oxblood fascia box with the flowing white script and its long tail, recessed downlights under it, cream piers banded in brown; inside, shelf on shelf of spines with a face-out row along the top, the red a[…]" (cut short in the record; the red carpet is a guess from it).

**Accuracy level:** 3 for the front. The inside follows the record's description.

**Guessed:** the depth (20 m of the 28 m stall), the red carpet, the three gondolas, the table of new titles, the counter by the door.

**Frame.** An 8 m front facing south onto the hall at z = 48 (x −110 to −102); the store runs north. Map tiles x 93–96, y 114–123.

## Front
- A deep oxblood fascia box with "B. Dalton" in flowing white script (Mr Dafoe, OFL) and BOOKSELLER in small capitals, with recessed downlights under it.
- Cream piers banded in brown, and an open front.

## Inside (walkable)
- Red carpet, cream walls.
- Wood bays of books to 2.3 m along both walls.
- Three double-sided gondolas of books, a table of new titles by the door, and the counter.
- **The counter (SHOPS, Nov 3, 2026; `design/godot-shops.md`).** It stood against the left wall's bays, with no room behind it. It now stands 1.1 m off the wall (its back; it was 0.5) and starts 2.0 m in from the front (was 2.5), so the attendant stands behind it; the clerks' aisle is an obstacle (shoppers can't walk behind it). World: x −108.78 to −108.16, z 43.6 to 46.0. Wing 2 (1995) needs a rebake.
- **Selling (SHOPS):** books are bought from the attendant ($10 each). The three Shiverwood paperbacks on the table of new titles are stock on show now, not pick-ups.
